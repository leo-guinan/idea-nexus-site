# The AI audit method

The audit is the discovery layer of an enterprise relationship, but it is not a disguised sales demo. It is a paid decision instrument.

## Five passes

1 — Inventory the initiatives, tools, promises, and anxieties already in motion.

2 — Map how work actually happens, including handoffs, delays, judgment, and invisible labor.

3 — Identify the leverage points: where a small intervention could change throughput, risk, quality, or coordination.

4 — Test AI fit against data boundaries, human control, governance, and operational readiness.

5 — Recommend one bounded intervention, including a clear do-not-build or not-ready finding when appropriate.

## The output

The client receives an organizational map, a prioritized opportunity set, an AI-fit assessment, governance boundaries, and a pilot brief with success criteria.

The audit does not promise transformation. It makes the next decision less stupid.
