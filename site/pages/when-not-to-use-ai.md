# When not to use AI

AI is not a maturity ritual. Sometimes the right answer is no.

The wrong intervention usually appears when the organization has not yet named the bottleneck. A model can produce activity, summaries, and dashboards while leaving the actual constraint untouched.

## Do not start with AI when

- ownership is unclear;
- the process is broken but undocumented;
- the source data cannot be trusted;
- the decision rights are contested;
- a conventional tool solves the problem more simply;
- the work is too infrequent to justify integration;
- nobody can say what success would look like.

## What to do instead

Map the work. Name the decision. Find the point where value is lost. Then compare interventions: process change, conventional automation, a purchased tool, a HumAIn system, or no change.

The audit is successful when the organization has a better decision—not when it has acquired another AI system.

## Falsifier

If an AI intervention cannot produce a measurable improvement in a bounded workflow, it has not earned expansion.
