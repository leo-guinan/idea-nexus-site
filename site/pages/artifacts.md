# Public artifacts

A thesis means nothing without the artifact.

This library will hold source-preserving examples of how Idea Nexus works: audit outputs, organizational maps, pilot scorecards, network diagrams, market theses, and outcome logs.

## What every artifact should show

- what was observed;
- what was inferred;
- what remains unknown;
- what action was taken;
- what changed;
- what would falsify the conclusion.

The first artifacts are being prepared. Until then, the method pages are the available evidence of the operating discipline.
