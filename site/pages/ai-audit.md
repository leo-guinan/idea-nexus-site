# The AI audit

Before buying another AI system, determine whether the problem is actually suitable for AI.

The audit is a paid decision instrument, not a generic strategy conversation and not a disguised promise that the answer will always be “more AI.”

## What we examine

- Current AI initiatives and hype bubbles
- Executive goals and operational priorities
- How work actually moves through the organization
- Where context, trust, and institutional memory are lost
- The people who can champion or block a change
- Data, privacy, security, and governance boundaries
- Whether a network-aware system is justified

## What you receive

- Executive decision memo
- Organizational and workflow map
- Leverage-point analysis
- AI-fit assessment
- Recommended intervention
- Explicit non-goals and risks
- Bounded pilot design
- Success measures and falsifiers

## The possible conclusion

The audit may recommend a HumAIn implementation. It may recommend a simpler tool. It may recommend process work first. It may recommend doing nothing yet.

That is the point.
