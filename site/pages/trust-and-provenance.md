# Trust and provenance

Trust is not a tone of voice. It is the ability to inspect how a claim became actionable.

A production AI system should make it possible to ask:

- What source supports this?
- When was it observed?
- Who or what transformed it?
- What uncertainty remains?
- Who is responsible for acting on it?

## Provenance is part of the product

Citations pasted onto the end of an answer are not enough if the system cannot preserve the relationship between source, interpretation, decision, and outcome.

That relationship is the receipt.

## The boundary

Provenance does not mean exposing private information. It means preserving the right evidence at the right boundary, with explicit access and retention rules.

A system that is helpful but impossible to audit is not ready for consequential work.
