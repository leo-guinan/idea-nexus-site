## Overview

Quiet Signal replaces the previous Hitchhiker-inspired expedition aesthetic with a calm, evidence-led research-office language. It uses warm paper, mineral accents, thin rules, restrained network diagrams, and editorial spacing.

## Colors

The neutral paper and ink system is shared across the site. Product, transformation, and research accents identify the three business lines without turning them into separate brands.

## Typography

A system sans keeps the interface legible and operational. Longer essays may introduce a serif editorial layer later; the first release stays deliberately spare.

## Layout

Use wide margins, a narrow reading column, asymmetric two-column sections, and thin rules instead of heavy cards.

## Do's and Don'ts

- Do show evidence, boundaries, and falsifiers.
- Do use small amounts of category color.
- Do not use neon gradients, robot imagery, glowing neural webs, or generic AI futurism.
- Do not make the visitor decode the thesis before they understand the offer.
